How small ideas become meaningful work

A five-minute field note · August 27

Good ideas rarely arrive as complete plans. They show up as a sentence, a question, or a detail that stays with us.

The useful habit is not collecting everything. It is noticing what has energy, then giving it a place to grow.

A quiet capture tool protects that fragile first step. The note can stay small today and still become something larger tomorrow.
